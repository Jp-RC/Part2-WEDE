WetNose is registered non-profit animal welfare organisation.

OUR MISSION AND VISION

Vision

We prioritising the well-being of the animals and only consider euthanasia when it it necessary to relieve their suffering.

Mission

To rescue animals, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned, save the abused, and neglected animals.
