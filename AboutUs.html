About Us

HISTORY

  • WetNose Animal Rescue Centre has beenrescuing animals and helping them find loving homes since July 1999.
  • The welfare centre cares for over 600 animals and provides them with a safe place to stay until they find their forever homes.
  • The WetNose Equestrian Centre opened in October 2019 to help farm animals.
  • WetNose has opened an Animal Clinic that providess affordable pet care while using its profits to help fund the organisation and support animals at the shelter.
